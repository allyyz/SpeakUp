Privacy & Data Policy

Your voice matters. Speaking up is never weakness - it's courage.

Confidentiality. Your identity is kept confidential and is only accessed when necessary for an authorized investigation.

What we collect. Your name, email, and the details and evidence you submit with a concern.

Who can see it. Only authorized HR administrators. Reports and Trends use aggregated data only.

How it is used. To review, investigate, and resolve workplace concerns, and to improve workplace policies and support.

Your rights. You can track the status of your reports at any time.
