Privacy & Data

How your information is protected

Confidentiality. Employee identities are hidden from HR by default and only revealed when necessary for an authorized investigation.

Access logging. Every time an identity is revealed, it is recorded in the case's access log.

Aggregated reporting. Reports & Trends shows counts only, never names.

Limited access. Only authorized HR administrators can view submitted concerns.

Your rights. Employees can track the status of their own reports at any time.
